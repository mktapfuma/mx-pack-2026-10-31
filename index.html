The value, in your numbers

We do not know your enquiry volume, your margin or your desk cost. You do. So the worksheet does the arithmetic with your figures, in your currency.

"If the number you get is larger than your monthly volume, this is not worth doing yet — and I would rather tell you that now than take your money."

Six inputs, one break-even figure, and two measures we will take in week one against a dated baseline: time to first response and the share of enquiries lost before a conversation starts.

What we will not promise you

  • No chat robot before there is real data behind it.
  • No computer-sent customer reply without a person reading it.
  • No model writes your prices, transit times or coverage.
  • No invented reviews, testimonials or performance figures.
  • No arrival-date prediction early — it is gated on measured accuracy against your own transit times.
  • No legal or compliance conclusion. What the Zambian Data Protection Act requires of your business is your lawyer's determination.
  • No scope change by conversation, and no prepayment buys future work.
  • No training or fine-tuning on your data.

The five things we need from you

Each one unlocks a specific part of the work.

  1. Which published number takes orders, and which is the fallback.
  2. The reply time your desk can hold — for example "same working day".
  3. WordPress editor access, this week.
  4. The name of the one person who signs off what is true about the business.
  5. Which product the tracking discovery is designed around: parcel tracking only, or parcel tracking plus vehicle-fleet visibility.
