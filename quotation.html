4 · When the work starts and when this quotation expires

Client inputs and commencement. Phase 1 begins on receipt of the last of these seven inputs:

  1. WordPress editor access
  2. hosting control
  3. staging access
  4. the order-taking number and its fallback number
  5. the response standard
  6. the named factual approver
  7. the client facts required for the twelve pages

Receipt of part of these inputs does not commence Phase 1 and does not start the delivery clock. If all of them have not arrived, the fixed fee is unchanged and the Phase 1 start date slides accordingly.

Quotation date: the date this quotation is signed. Valid through: the date 30 calendar days after the quotation date. The client-input commencement condition does not extend, renew or suspend this validity period.

5 · What this quotation does not include

  1. Phase 2 — channel wiring, owned social publishing, organic foundations, transactional email and the monthly reporting dashboard.
  2. Phase 3 — parcel tracking or vehicle-fleet visibility, including the discovery session, data model, integrations, build, controls, notifications and pilot. No code before the discovery gate.
  3. A host, tracking product or licence estimate. Discovery output must exist first and is then priced separately.
  4. Any third-party cost (see §2).
  5. A support or maintenance retainer after acceptance.
  6. Legal advice, a tax opinion, a compliance conclusion, an insurance opinion, or any representation of what Zambian or South African law requires of you.
  7. Content facts, proof points, coverage, fleet size, transit times, service levels, prices or phone numbers that you have not supplied and approved.
  8. Any exclusivity, non-circumvention, minimum spend, future-work promise, discount against a later phase, or right to use your marks beyond the accepted deliverable.

6 · Both sides' commitments

  • Before you accept: we have performed no billable work under this quotation and hold no pass-through float.
  • Reversibility: a verified full backup, database export and documented rollback path precede the first live change.
  • Scope change: no phase price changes by oral implication. A change is priced in a new written instrument and agreed before it is delivered.
  • No prepayment buys future work. A deposit, if one is agreed, buys no exclusivity and no promise of a later phase.
  • Revocation: MVEOS may revoke the authority to issue this quotation in writing before you accept it. Revocation does not erase an already accepted contract.
  • Your inputs are on the critical path. Rate card, service list, lane list, size bands, proof points, document flow and consent wording are yours. If they are not supplied, the work waits — the fee does not change and the start date slides.
