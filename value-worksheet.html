What this worksheet deliberately does not do

  • No invented saving. No "save 30% of admin time" figure is asserted anywhere.

  • No discount, no bundle, no free pilot.
  • No compliance conclusion. What the Zambian Data Protection Act requires of your business is your lawyer's determination.
